Services

The
Disciplines

Three practices share one atelier in Beirut. They share the same hands, the same standards of finishing, and the same refusal to hurry.

Every commission is private. Nothing shown here is held in stock.

I

Couture

Made to measure

A garment drawn for one body and one occasion. The commission opens with a conversation about the event, the light, the way you wish to move — then a toile, then as many fittings as the piece requires.

  • Private consultation and sketch
  • Toile and three to five fittings
  • Hand embroidery, beading and finishing
  • Bridal, evening and ceremonial dress
  • Eight to sixteen weeks from first fitting

II

Prêt-à-Porter

Seasonal, in limited numbers

A short seasonal line cut from the same cloth houses as the couture, in a small run of sizes. Pieces are finished by the same atelier and may be altered to the client.

  • Limited seasonal line
  • Day, cocktail and resort dress
  • Alterations included
  • Private viewing in Beirut or by appointment abroad
  • Immediate to four weeks

III

Fine Jewelry

Commissioned and archival

Pieces made to accompany a garment, or to stand alone. Stones are selected with the client; settings are drawn and executed by hand in gold and platinum.

  • Stone selection with the client
  • Hand-drawn settings in gold and platinum
  • Bridal and ceremonial commissions
  • Restoration of family pieces
  • Six to twelve weeks

The Process

Four rooms,
in order.

01

The Conversation

We meet, privately. Nothing is measured yet — we speak about the occasion and what the piece must do.

02

The Drawing

A sketch and a cloth selection are presented. Revisions are expected, and welcome.

03

The Fittings

A toile first, then the cloth. The piece is corrected on the body until it is correct.

04

The Delivery

Pressed, boxed and accompanied by its care instructions. Worldwide delivery by arrangement.
