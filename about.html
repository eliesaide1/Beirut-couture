About

The
Maison

Beirut Couture was founded on a single conviction — that a garment should be made for one person, once, and made entirely by hand.

We do not produce collections on a calendar. We receive a small number of clients each season, and we give each of them the whole of our attention.

I. The Hand

Everything that
can be done by hand,
is.

Seams are closed by hand. Embroidery is worked on a frame, thread by thread, by people who have done nothing else for twenty years. A beaded bodice can take six weeks on its own.

Machines are used where a machine is genuinely better, and nowhere else. The distinction matters more than the romance of it: a hand-rolled hem moves differently, and the body can tell.

II. The House

A house built on
the hand, not the season.

01

Beirut

The atelier has always been here. The city's light, its stone and its appetite for occasion are in the work whether we intend it or not.

02

One room

Drawing, fitting and finishing happen under one roof. Nothing is sent out, so nothing is explained twice.

03

Few clients

We take on as many commissions as can be done properly, and then we stop. It is the only way the above stays true.
