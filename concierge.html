Concierge

At Your
Service

A commission does not end when the piece is delivered. The atelier keeps every pattern it has ever cut, and remains available to the people it was cut for.

What follows is offered to clients of the house, without charge unless the work is substantial.

I

Appointments

Beirut, or wherever you are

Fittings take place at the atelier by default. Where an occasion makes that impractical, a fitter travels — Paris, London, the Gulf and further by arrangement.

  • Private consultation, by appointment only
  • Travelling fittings by arrangement
  • Evening and weekend appointments for clients abroad
  • Discretion as a matter of course

II

Wardrobe Care

For the life of the piece

Couture is not dry-cleaned like anything else. Beading, hand-rolled edges and structured foundations each want different handling, and most of it we would rather do ourselves.

  • Cleaning and pressing by specialists we appoint
  • Re-fitting as the body changes
  • Storage advice, and boxing for travel
  • Repair of beading and embroidery

III

Archive & Restoration

Including pieces that are not ours

Every pattern is kept. A piece made for a mother can be remade for a daughter, in her measurements, in the same cloth where the cloth still exists.

We also restore family pieces of other houses, where the work is within our hands to do honestly.

  • Patterns retained indefinitely
  • Remaking from the archive
  • Restoration of heirloom dress and jewelry
  • Written condition report before any work begins
